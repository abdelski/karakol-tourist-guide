Karakol & Issyk-Kul — What To Do

Guest information board · September & October · Updated 2026

Before You Go

  • Exchange rate: ~87 KGS = $1 USD (Sep 2026)
  • Cash only in mountains — withdraw in Karakol
  • Yandex Go app works for taxis in town
  • Pack: layers, rain shell, down jacket, headlamp, water filter/tablets
  • Emergency: 112
  • Mountain rescue: +996 555 445 174
  • Tourist police: +996 705 009 102
  • Visitor Center: 150 Lenin St · Mon–Sat 9:00–18:00
Autumn note: Lower valleys (Jeti-Oguz, Altyn Arashan, Jyrgalan day hikes) are excellent in Sep–Oct. High passes above 3,500 m (especially Ala-Kul at 3,906 m) may have snow and closing camps — always confirm conditions before you leave.

In Karakol (Half Day – Full Day)

City Walk — Mosques, Cathedral, Bazaars

FreeYear-round

Suggested route: Dungan Mosque → Russian Quarter → Tatar Mosque → Holy Trinity Cathedral → History Museum → Bugu Bazaar → ashlyan-fu lunch.

  • Ashlyan-fu (cold Dungan noodle soup): ~150–250 KGS at stalls near the bazaar
  • Guided walking tour: book via Destination Karakol cultural tours

Sunday Animal Market (Mal Bazaar)

FreeSundays only · 7:00–11:00

Livestock market — horses, sheep, cattle. One of the most authentic experiences in Kyrgyzstan. Go early (by 7 AM).

How to get there:
Marshrutka #101 (~20 KGS) or Yandex taxi (~70 KGS) toward Jany-Aryk district (market relocated 2024 — confirm exact location with Visitor Center or your host).
Wear sturdy shoes — muddy and crowded.

More info: destinationkarakol.com → Markets

Day Trips from Karakol

Jeti-Oguz — Red Rocks & Kok-Jaiyk Valley

Best in autumnHalf / full day

Famous red sandstone cliffs ("Seven Bulls", "Broken Heart"), flower valley, optional hike to Kok-Jaiyk (6 km from resort). Entry ~50 KGS/person.

Option A — Public transport (cheapest):
1. Go to Ak-Tilek Bazaar bus area.
2. Take marshrutka #355 (~100 KGS, goes to resort) or #371 (~80 KGS, stops at Jeti-Oguz village).
3. Important: Tell driver "Zheti-Oguz Kurort" (Жети-Огуз Курорт) — the resort is 12 km past the village.
4. If dropped at village: hitch or shared taxi ~500 KGS to Seven Bulls viewpoint.
Return: Last buses ~16:00–17:00 from resort. Shared taxi round-trip from Karakol: ~1,500–2,500 KGS/car.
Option B — Taxi: Yandex or negotiate ~800–1,200 KGS one way · ~1,500–2,500 KGS return with waiting time.

Guided horse trek (3 days): CBT Jeti-Oguz Highlights · May–Oct · book: +996 555 150 795

Skazka (Fairy Tale) Canyon

Half dayYear-round access

Wind-eroded red clay formations on Issyk-Kul south shore. ~2–3 hrs exploring on foot.

How to get there:
1. Go to 220 Derbishev Street (southern marshrutka stop).
2. Take marshrutka #315 or #310 toward Bokonbayevo / Balykchy.
3. Tell driver: "Skazka" or "Skazka Canyon" — drops you at turn-off on A363 highway (~1.5–2 hrs).
4. Walk or hitch 2 km up dirt road to gate · entry 50 KGS.
Fares: ~250–400 KGS each way · pay driver in cash.
Return: Flag down #315/#310 heading east toward Karakol — be back at main road by 15:00–16:00 (last buses ~17:00).
Taxi alternative: ~2,100 KGS one way from Karakol.

Barskoon Gorge & Waterfall

Full dayAutumn colors

Forest gorge with waterfall on south shore, ~90 km from Karakol. Often combined with Skazka Canyon.

Marshrutka #315 from 220 Derbishev St → get off at Barskoon village, then taxi/hitch into gorge (~30 min).
Or arrange private day taxi via Visitor Center (~4,000–6,000 KGS/car for Skazka + Barskoon combo).

Hiking & Hot Springs

Altyn Arashan — Hot Springs Valley

✅ Sep–Oct1–2 days2,435 m

Alpine valley with natural hot springs. Best autumn bet when high passes are risky. Trail: 15 km one way, +900 m, 5–7 hrs.

DIY hike — step by step:
1. Ak-Tilek Bazaar → marshrutka #350 (sign: "Ak-Suu Kurort" / Ак-Суу Курорт) · ~50 KGS · 20–25 min.
2. Tell driver to stop at Altyn Arashan turn-off — fork in road with valley sign (end of Ak-Suu village). Do NOT stay on bus to the sanatorium unless you want the arboretum trail instead.
3. Hike 15 km up the valley to hot springs area.
4. Hot spring soak: ~150–400 KGS · Valley overnight (yurt/camp on trail): ~1,000–3,500 KGS with meals.
5. Return hike next day, or 4×4 down: ~1,500–2,500 KGS · catch #350 back to Karakol.
Last marshrutka: be back at Ak-Suu road by ~17:00.
4×4 day trip (no hiking):
CBT "Altyn Arashan by 4WD" · 1 day · hot springs + short walks · Apr–Nov · +996 555 150 795

Side trip: From Altyn Arashan you can attempt Ala-Kul pass day hike (7–8 hrs, 3,900 m) — only if conditions confirmed clear.

Ala-Kul Lake Trek

⚠ Check conditions3–4 daysup to 3,906 m
Classic route crosses Ala-Kul Pass (3,906 m). Season is normally June–September. In late September / October: snow likely, yurt camps at Sirota may be closed. Confirm with Visitor Center before booking.

Route: Karakol Gorge → Sirota camp → Ala-Kul Lake (3,533 m) → Ala-Kul Pass → Altyn Arashan → Ak-Suu → Karakol. ~40 km total.

Trailhead (clockwise / standard start):
Marshrutka #101 from Karakol → last stop at Ala-Kul Nature Park gate (~30 KGS).
Park fee at gate: ~250 KGS/person.
Alternative start: #350 to Ak-Suu → Altyn Arashan → hike up to lake (anti-clockwise).

Jyrgalan Valley — Quieter Alternative to Ala-Kul

Day hikes OK in Oct60 km east

Marked trails, fewer crowds, golden larch in autumn. Multi-day treks officially June–September.

Karakol → Jyrgalan:
1. Ak-Tilek Bazaar (Bektenova & Zhamansariev intersection).
2. Marshrutka #331 — label may say "Jyrgalan Village" or "Shakta" (NOT the one that only goes to Boz Uchuk).
3. Departures: 08:30 · 14:00 · 17:30 (summer) · ~150–200 KGS · ~2 hrs.
4. Arrive 30 min early — buses fill up.
Jyrgalan → Karakol: 07:30 · 10:30 · 16:30 from village bus stop.
Private taxi: ~4,000–5,000 KGS/car · 1.5–2 hrs.

Suggested Itineraries (Sep – Oct)

3 Days

  1. City walk + food experience
  2. Jeti-Oguz day trip (marshrutka)
  3. Altyn Arashan overnight hike OR 4×4 day trip

5 Days

  1. City + cooking class or family dinner
  2. Jeti-Oguz
  3. Altyn Arashan overnight
  4. Skazka Canyon
  5. Sunday animal market (if Sunday)

7 Days

  1. Above 5-day plan
  2. Jyrgalan: 2 days (day hikes or 3-day trek if season allows)
  3. Buffer / Barskoon / horse ride in Jeti-Oguz
